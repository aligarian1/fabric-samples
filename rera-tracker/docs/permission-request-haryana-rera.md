**Subject:** Request for permission to reproduce public project information from the HRERA portal

To the Web Information Manager, HRERA Panchkula / Gurugram,

I am [your name], [business name / "an individual"], based in [city].

I am building a small service that helps people who have bought flats in
under-construction projects follow those projects. For each project a buyer
chooses, the service reads the public pages of the HRERA portal that are linked
openly from the portal's menus (the registered-projects list, a project's
details page and its quarterly progress reports) and sends the buyer a short
message, in English or Hindi, whenever something changes: a new quarterly
report, a new hearing entry, a change in the registration date, or a missing
quarterly report.

Each message:

* repeats only what the portal shows, as filed, without comment on the promoter;
* links to the official HRERA page;
* names the Haryana RERA portal as the source; and
* says that it is information and not legal advice.

The service reads pages one at a time with a pause of several seconds between
requests, identifies itself with a contact address, does not use the portal's
captcha-protected search, and does not log in.

Under the portal's Copyright Policy, material may be reproduced after taking
proper permission. I request your permission to reproduce this information in
the way described above. If any part of it is not acceptable (for example the
frequency of reading, or the use of the information in a paid service), please
tell me and I will change it. If an official data feed or API exists, I would
prefer to use that.

Thank you for your time.
